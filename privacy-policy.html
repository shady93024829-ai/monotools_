Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

MonoTools ("we", "the site") provides free, browser-based image tools. This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site.

Your files are never uploaded

Every tool on this site (QR generator, background remover, image compressor, profile picture maker, image-to-PDF) processes your files entirely on your own device using your browser's built-in capabilities. Your images, PDFs, and QR code content are never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage only to remember your language preference (English or Arabic) between visits. This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Third-party services (YouTube thumbnails)

The YouTube Thumbnail Downloader tool fetches publicly available thumbnail images directly from YouTube's own servers based on the video link you paste. This request goes directly from your browser to YouTube, not through us.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising networks. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization preferences at Google's Ads Settings page.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the email listed on our Contact page.
